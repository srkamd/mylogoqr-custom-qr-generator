## 4. Cannibalization Rules
1. **Never target "Free QR Code Generator with Logo" on secondary blog posts** — that intent belongs 100% to the Homepage (`/`).
2. **Blog articles must link back to their respective generator landing pages** using descriptive contextual anchor text (e.g. `[create a Wi-Fi QR code](/wifi-qr-code-generator)`).
3. **No meta keywords tags** are used on any page in accordance with Google Webmaster guidelines.
