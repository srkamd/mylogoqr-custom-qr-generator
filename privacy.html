Privacy Commitment

We believe privacy is a fundamental right. Your data never leaves your device.

100% secure client-side generation

100% On-Device Processing

MyLogoQR operates completely inside your web browser. When you paste website links, type network credentials, or enter Wi-Fi passwords, the data stays in your browser's local memory. The final QR code image and styling overlays are rendered directly using your machine's canvas CPU/GPU, without transmitting a single byte to a remote database or cloud server.

Zero Tracking or Logs

We do not log the text payloads you input, we do not count how many codes you generate, and we do not track scan conversions. There are no advertising identifiers or marketing cookies tracking your habits. Your codes, credentials, and brand logo files remain entirely confidential.

Secure CDN Delivery

To ensure blazing fast loading speeds, MyLogoQR pulls standard styling files (Tailwind CSS) and open-source helper libraries (Lucide Icons, jsPDF) from secure, distributed Content Delivery Networks. These servers may log regular request headers (like IP addresses and browser types) to securely serve files, which is governed under their standard web asset delivery policies.

Absolute Transparency

Because MyLogoQR runs on pure client-side HTML and JavaScript, the source code is open and fully inspectable. Anyone can open their browser's Developer Tools (F12) -> Network panel to verify that the app does not send outbound data while you type or customize your codes.

If you have questions regarding our local execution architecture, please write to us at support@mylogoqr.com .
