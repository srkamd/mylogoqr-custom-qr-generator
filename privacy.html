Privacy Policy & Data Security

Our privacy philosophy is simple: we cannot lose, sell, track, or compromise your data because we never receive it in the first place.

  • Zero Server Logging
  • Zero Database Storage
  • GDPR & CCPA Compliant by Design
  • In-Memory Processing
PData Protection Notice • Effective Date: October 2026

1. The Zero-Knowledge Client-Side Principle

Unlike conventional web applications that transmit form entries to a remote server for processing, MyLogoQR is built with a zero-knowledge, client-side architecture.

Every QR code calculation, cryptographic Wi-Fi payload construction, vCard contact formatting, and PNG/SVG rendering occurs 100% within your local web browser's memory using JavaScript and the HTML5 Canvas API. No sensitive data—including Wi-Fi network passwords, personal cell numbers, website URLs, or uploaded logos—ever traverses the network or touches our servers.

2. What We Collect (And What We Don't)

✅ What Never Leaves Your Device

  • URLs, text notes, and serialized data entered into the generator.
  • Wi-Fi network names (SSID) and network access security keys.
  • vCard contact cards (names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses).
  • Image files and brand logos uploaded for QR code compositing.

ℹ️ Technical Telemetry (Web Standards)

  • Standard static HTTP web server access logs (IP address, user agent, requested static file URL) required by edge CDNs to defend against DDoS attacks.
  • Client-side browser localStorage used solely to remember your preferred UI theme (dark or light mode).

3. Third-Party Advertising & Cookies (Google AdSense)

To fund the edge hosting and server bandwidth of MyLogoQR without charging user subscription fees, we display advertisements via Google AdSense.

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other sites on the web. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings or the Network Advertising Initiative.

4. Contact Form Inquiries

If you choose to contact us via the form on our Contact Page, your submitted name, email address, and inquiry message are routed directly to Shahrukh Ahmad (support@mylogoqr.com) via encrypted transport for the sole purpose of replying to your inquiry. We never add your email to marketing newsletters or sell it to third parties.

5. Security of Generated Static Barcodes

Because generated QR codes are static and link directly to your target destination, they cannot be manipulated, redirected, or intercepted after download. We strongly advise users to inspect and test-scan codes before printing high-volume materials.
