Privacy Policy & Data Security

Our privacy philosophy is simple: we cannot lose, sell, or compromise your data because we never receive it in the first place.

1. Zero Server-Side Data Storage

MyLogoQR operates completely within your local web browser. All QR code matrix calculations, shape styling, logo rendering, and PNG/SVG export downloads happen on your own computer or smartphone CPU. No input data (including URLs, Wi-Fi passwords, contact details, or uploaded logo images) is ever uploaded to our servers.

2. Cookies & Local Storage

We use minimal browser localStorage only to preserve non-identifiable user interface preferences (such as your preferred light/dark theme). We do not set tracking cookies or create cross-site behavioral profiles.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Services

We display third-party advertisements via Google AdSense to fund our hosting infrastructure. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to our website or other sites on the internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings (adssettings.google.com).

4. Updates to this Policy

Any updates to our privacy policy will be posted on this page with a revised modification date. Last updated: October 2026.
