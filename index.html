Understanding QR Code Error Correction (L, M, Q, H)

QR codes possess built-in data redundancy, known as Reed-Solomon Error Correction. This technology enables barcodes to be read even if parts of the print are torn, dirty, or covered by a custom logo overlay. Selecting the correct error correction level is critical for design compliance:

Error Correction Level Data Recovery Capability Recommended Design Use Case
Level L (Low) Recovers up to 7% of missing data Best for raw text with no logo overlays (cleanest, least dense pattern).
Level M (Medium) Recovers up to 15% of missing data Default setting. Good for tiny logo overlays or simple background gradients.
Level Q (Quartile) Recovers up to 25% of missing data Recommended for standard business card logo overlays (strong contrast).
Level H (High) Recovers up to 30% of missing data Essential for large custom logos, dense vCard datasets, or prints placed outdoors.

Design Tip: Higher error correction levels increase the density of the code. If your QR code has a very high data density (such as a long vCard), make sure to increase the export resolution slider to 1000px before downloading to prevent printing fuzziness.

Export Formats: Scalable Vector SVG vs Raster PNG/PDF

Choosing the correct export format ensures your printed QR codes render beautifully across multiple mediums:

Vector SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics)

Vector files store the barcode as geometric paths. They do not lose resolution when scaled, making them essential for billboard signage, packaging, and commercial printers.

Raster PNG (Portable Network Graphics)

PNG is a lossless image format. Ideal for digital media, social cards, email signatures, and web pages where simple image imports are required.

Document PDF (Portable Document Format)

PDF packages the layout with margins and text, making it perfect for printing templates directly on standard A4 or Letter papers for storefront displays.

No. Static QR codes encode data values directly in their physical patterns. If you need to edit the target URL or phone details, you must generate a new code. We offer this service completely free with no restrictions.
