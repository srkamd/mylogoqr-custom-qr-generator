1. The Vision Behind MyLogoQR

Too many utilities on the modern web have become predatory: locking basic functionality behind credit card paywalls, expiring user links after 14 days, or silently tracking scan locations and IP addresses.

Shahrukh Ahmad conceived and engineered MyLogoQR with a single foundational goal: reclaim utility privacy. By delegating all Reed-Solomon polynomial math, canvas rasterization, and vector path generation directly to the client browser, users receive high-fidelity, permanent QR codes with absolute data confidentiality.

2. Our Editorial Guidelines & Content Integrity

The MyLogoQR Knowledge Hub was established to educate developers, designers, small business owners, and tech enthusiasts on modern software engineering, QR mechanics, AI agent workflows, and digital marketing.

Our 4 Core Editorial Commitments:

  • First-Party Technical Verification: Every code snippet, algorithm explanation, and deployment guideline is tested and verified by our engineering team before publication.
  • Zero Sensationalism: We present technological capabilities—including AI automation, SaaS building, and computer vision—with pragmatic realism, clear trade-offs, and ethical disclosures.
  • Continuous Re-Auditing: Technology moves quickly. Articles are periodically reviewed and updated to reflect current API revisions, algorithm updates, and security standards.
  • Complete Independence: Editorial opinions and product reviews are completely independent of sponsored placement or affiliate bias.

3. Technical Philosophy

We believe in the power of client-side computing. Modern devices possess tremendous GPU and JavaScript rendering performance that eliminates the need to route sensitive user data through centralized web servers. This philosophy underpins every tool we build and every article we publish.
