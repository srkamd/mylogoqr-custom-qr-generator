The Vision: Free, Private Utilities for Everyone

MyLogoQR was born out of frustration with online QR utilities that demand credit cards, limit scans to 50 clicks, or quietly redirect printed flyers to broken URLs once a 14-day trial concludes.

We believe basic digital utilities should be as reliable and permanent as physical paper. A QR code is an optical representation of text—there is no technical reason why generating one should require a remote database or a monthly subscription fee.

Architecture & Privacy by Design

Our entire application is built using modern client-side standards. When you enter a link, phone number, or Wi-Fi password, the browser canvas renders the vector matrix directly inside local memory. Your data never touches an external server, eliminating the possibility of data breaches, third-party selling, or tracking.
