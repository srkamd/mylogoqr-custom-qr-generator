Welcome to MyLogoQR! MyLogoQR was conceptualized and engineered by full-stack developer Shahrukh Ahmad (SRK AMD). Shahrukh noticed a growing need for a highly customizable, private, and developer-friendly QR code utility that respects user data.

Most QR code generators on the internet hide advanced features behind expensive monthly subscriptions, track user scans on external servers, or inject advertising redirects. MyLogoQR solves all of these problems by running 100% client-side inside your web browser. No data ever leaves your device, ensuring maximum security and absolute privacy for your personal credentials, Wi-Fi networks, and contact vCards.

As a senior software engineer and QA practitioner, Shahrukh built MyLogoQR with high-resolution canvas layout composition, dual-tone custom gradients, preset vector logos, and customizable outer frame outlines suitable for print-ready downloads (PNG, vector SVG, and PDF layouts).
