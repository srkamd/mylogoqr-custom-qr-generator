Why Was MyLogoQR Created?

The internet is filled with QR code generators, but a closer look reveals a worrying trend: subscription traps and privacy violations. Most commercial generators claim to offer "free" codes, only to deactivate them after a few scans or lock them behind a monthly paywall. They do this by forcing your QR code to link to their redirection servers (dynamic QR codes), giving them total control over your traffic.

Additionally, server-side generators process your confidential details (such as Wi-Fi passwords, contact phone numbers, and private text notes) on external database machines. This opens doors to data logging and leaks.

We built MyLogoQR to provide a 100% free, client-side alternative. By utilizing local JavaScript processing in your browser, MyLogoQR compiles your barcodes entirely on your own device. No form data is ever transmitted to a database. The result is a static, raw data QR code that is permanently yours, will never expire, has no scan limits, and is completely free of charge.

How Do We Benefit from Running This Free Site?

A common question is: "If the service is completely free and runs client-side, what does the creator get?"

Our business model is built on transparency. By keeping server resource consumption near zero (since your browser does the heavy lifting of rendering codes), we can run this global platform with minimal overhead. We monetize this tool through:

  • Display Advertisements (Google AdSense): We place clean, non-intrusive ad slots to monetize high-intent organic search traffic.
  • Developer Showcase: This platform serves as a production-grade demonstration of full-stack client-side engineering, helping us win software development contracts.

Step-by-Step Instructions: Creating Custom Barcodes

1. Choose Your QR Data Type

Select the tab matching your goal: Website Link, WhatsApp Direct Chat, Wi-Fi Network Access, Digital vCard Contact, or Static Plain Text. Each tab structures data using international protocol standards.

2. Customize Colors & Gradients

Select solid or dual-tone linear and radial gradients. Always ensure high contrast between your dark pattern and light background so phone cameras can distinguish modules in dim light.

3. Upload Brand Logo

Upload your logo in PNG, JPEG, or SVG format under 5 MB. Adjust the logo size slider between 15% and 25% to ensure the barcode matrix retains adequate scannable data redundancy.

4. Select Error Correction Level

If adding a logo, always choose Error Correction Level Q (25%) or Level H (30%). This tells the Reed-Solomon math engine to build redundant data blocks around your logo image.

5. Download Print-Ready High-Res Formats

Export in 1000 × 1000 px PNG for web, email signatures, and social media, or vector SVG for commercial printing presses, billboards, and product packaging.
