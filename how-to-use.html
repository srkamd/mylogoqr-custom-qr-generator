4. Printing & Design Best Practices

To ensure maximum scannability and professional presentation, always observe these three core design principles when compiling your QR codes:

  • Contrast is Key: Always keep the foreground dots color significantly darker than the background canvas. Light gray dots on white backgrounds will fail to scan on budget phone cameras.
  • Logo Scale: Keep the logo size slider between 15% and 30%. QR codes have built-in error correction, but if a logo is too large, it will cover vital data blocks and break the scan.
  • Print Formats: For print designs, download the vector **SVG** format. SVG files do not lose quality or pixelate when stretched, making them perfect for large banner printing. Use **PNG** for digital portfolios and web pages.

5. FAQ & Troubleshooting Guide

Why isn't my QR code scanning?

Check if the colors have enough contrast. If you uploaded a custom logo, try lowering the logo scale slider to 20% to free up blocked data modules.

Can I change my static QR details later?

Static QR codes encode the data directly inside the pattern. To change the details, you must generate a new QR code (which is always 100% free and takes 5 seconds on our homepage).
