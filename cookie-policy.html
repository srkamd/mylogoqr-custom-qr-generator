1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text fragments sent to your web browser by a website you visit. They help the website remember information about your visit, such as your preferred display options, making your next visit easier and the site more functional.

2. Does the MyLogoQR Generator Use Cookies?

No. Our core QR code generation engine is 100% cookie-free and client-side.

When you enter URLs, configure Wi-Fi credentials, format vCards, or upload brand logos, no cookies are issued, and no session identifiers are exchanged with our server. To remember your local interface preferences (such as light or dark display mode), we utilize browser localStorage, which remains entirely on your personal machine and is never transmitted across the network.

3. Third-Party Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense)

To keep MyLogoQR completely free, non-expiring, and accessible to everyone worldwide without mandatory paid subscriptions, we partner with third-party advertising networks, primarily Google AdSense.

Google AdSense Cookie Disclosures:

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve advertisements on https://mylogoqr.com.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites across the internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google My Ad Center or Google Ad Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

5. How You Can Manage & Disable Cookies

You can configure or adjust your web browser settings at any time to reject all cookies or notify you whenever a cookie is set. Please consult your browser's official instructions:

  • Google Chrome Cookie Settings
  • Mozilla Firefox Cookie Settings
  • Apple Safari Cookie Settings
  • Microsoft Edge Cookie Settings
