How to use WhatsApp QR codes for orders & support

MWritten by MyLogoQR Editorial Team — Last updated October 2026

WhatsApp is the world's most popular messaging app with over 2 billion active users. When customers want support, inquiries, or quick orders, they prefer sending a chat message over filling out long contact forms or waiting on hold on the telephone.

Formatting your phone number correctly

Always input your phone number in full international format without plus signs, spaces, or leading zeros. For example, if your number is in the UK with country code +44, enter 447123456789. In Pakistan (+92), enter 923001234567. In the US (+1), enter 15551234567.

Using prefilled messages

Prefilled messages give customers a frictionless starting point. For restaurant takeaway orders, use: "Hello! I'd like to place an order from table 5." For service inquiries, use: "Hi, I saw your flyer and would like a free consultation quote."

Pro tip: Place your WhatsApp QR code on delivery packaging, store receipts, and checkout counter displays to turn one-time shoppers into repeat buyers.

Frequently asked questions

Does the customer need to save my number first?

No! The official WhatsApp wa.me API link allows anyone to chat with you immediately without adding you to their phone address book.

Can I add my business logo to the WhatsApp QR code?

Yes, upload your logo or the official WhatsApp icon in PNG or SVG format. Error correction Q ensures the code remains 100% scannable.

Will my WhatsApp QR code ever expire?

No. These are permanent static QR codes that link directly to your WhatsApp account without third-party redirects.
