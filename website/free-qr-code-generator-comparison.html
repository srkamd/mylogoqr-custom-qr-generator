⚠️ The "Free Trial Redirect Trap" Explained

The most common complaint in the QR code industry stems from services that disguise 14-day dynamic redirect trials as "free QR codes". Here is how the trap works:

  1. A user searches for "free QR code generator" and creates a code for their restaurant menu, business card, or wedding flyer.
  2. The generator encodes an intermediary tracking URL (e.g., https://l.ead.me/xyz) rather than the direct destination.
  3. The user prints 1,000 brochures, acrylic signs, or stickers.
  4. Two weeks later, the free trial ends. Anyone scanning the printed QR code is redirected to a billing paywall demanding a recurring subscription to reactivate the link.
The Golden Rule: Always check the raw decoded payload before sending files to print. If the scan URL starts with an unfamiliar third-party domain rather than your direct website or WiFi payload, it is a dynamic redirect that will cease functioning if an account lapses.

🎯 Selecting the Right Tool for Your Project

Choose MyLogoQR When:

  • Permanent Print Runs: You are printing packaging, business cards, signage, or table tents that must scan reliably for years without maintenance fees.
  • Sensitive Credentials: You are generating guest Wi-Fi passwords, vCard contact information, or direct WhatsApp chats and want mathematical client-side privacy.
  • Commercial Branding: You want custom brand colors, corner eye styling, and your company logo placed cleanly in the center with Reed-Solomon Level H protection.
  • Zero Bureaucracy: You need an instant high-resolution SVG or PNG download without creating logins, entering credit cards, or subscribing to marketing emails.

Choose a Paid Dynamic Service (Bitly / QR Tiger) When:

  • URL Updates are Necessary: You frequently change promotional landing pages but cannot afford to reprint physical billboard posters or product packaging.
  • Granular Analytics: Your marketing team requires daily scan counts, GPS coordinate approximations, device operating system breakdowns, and referrer data.
  • Retargeting Pixels: You want to trigger Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel, or LinkedIn Insight tags whenever a customer scans your code in store.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can a static QR code from MyLogoQR ever stop working?

No. Static QR codes are identical to printed text. As long as your destination link remains online and the printed QR code remains physically unscratched and readable, it will work forever.

Why does Canva require a paid plan for vector QR code downloads?

Canva restricts vector SVG and transparent PDF exports to Canva Pro subscribers. MyLogoQR provides uncompressed, mathematically exact vector SVG downloads for free on every generator type.

What is the safest error correction level for adding logos?

Level H (High) is recommended whenever placing a logo in the center of a QR code. Level H allows up to 30% of the barcode area to be covered or damaged while maintaining reliable scanning performance.

Does MyLogoQR log my scan count?

No. Because MyLogoQR generates static QR codes that connect scanners directly to your target destination, no scans ever route through our servers. We have zero knowledge of how many times your codes are scanned.
