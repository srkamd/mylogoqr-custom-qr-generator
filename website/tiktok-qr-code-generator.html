Supported TikTok Link Formats

MyLogoQR handles all standard TikTok web protocols and deep-links:

  • Profile Links: https://www.tiktok.com/@username — Opens your full creator profile with your bio, follow button, and video grid.
  • Specific Viral Videos: https://www.tiktok.com/@username/video/1234567890... — Routes scanners directly to a specific tutorial, product demo, or challenge.
  • TikTok Live Streams: https://www.tiktok.com/@username/live — Lets event attendees jump straight into your live broadcast in real time.

Designing High-Converting TikTok QR Codes

A plain black-and-white square looks like a generic shipping barcode. To maximize scan rates:

  1. Brand with Official Colors: Use TikTok's high-contrast color palette — jet black (#010101), electric cyan (#25F4EE), and neon pink (#FE2C55) — on a crisp white background.
  2. Embed Your Creator Avatar or TikTok Glyph: Upload your channel logo or select the TikTok music note icon. Our generator automatically sets Error Correction Level H (30%) so your code remains instantly scannable even with a centered graphic.
  3. Always Include an Actionable CTA: Frame your code with clear text such as "Scan for Exclusive Behind-the-Scenes on TikTok" or "Follow on TikTok for Daily Giveaways".
Print Vector Recommendation: For apparel tags, vehicle wraps, and trade show banners, always export in SVG format. Vector SVGs scale infinitely with zero pixelation or blurry edges at 300+ DPI.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get my direct TikTok profile link?

Open the TikTok app, tap your Profile tab in the bottom right, tap the three-line hamburger menu in the top right, select 'Share profile', and tap 'Copy link'. Your link will look like https://www.tiktok.com/@yourusername.

Will scanning this QR code open the TikTok app directly?

Yes! Modern iOS and Android camera apps automatically recognize tiktok.com URLs and prompt users to launch the official TikTok native app directly to your profile, video, or live stream.

Can I make a QR code for a specific TikTok video or Live stream?

Yes. On any TikTok video or live stream, tap the Share arrow icon, copy the link, and paste it into MyLogoQR. The generated QR code will lead scanners straight to that exact video or active broadcast.

What is the recommended size to print a TikTok QR code on merchandise or stickers?

For clothing tags, stickers, and business cards, print at a minimum of 1 inch by 1 inch (25mm x 25mm). For event posters, stage banners, or storefront decals, print at 2 to 4 inches (50mm - 100mm) using our vector SVG export for crisp scannability.

Do these TikTok QR codes ever expire or stop working?

No. MyLogoQR generates permanent, static QR codes that encode your official TikTok web address directly into the barcode pattern. There are no middleman redirect servers, no expiration dates, and no monthly fees.
