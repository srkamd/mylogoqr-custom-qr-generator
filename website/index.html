Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed technical and practical answers to common questions about QR generation, commercial usage, and data security.

Can I change or edit a QR code after printing it?

No. Codes generated on MyLogoQR are permanent static QR codes. The encoded data (such as your website URL, contact information, or Wi-Fi password) is directly compiled into the physical matrix module pattern. Because there is no intermediary redirect server or database lookup, the destination cannot be altered once printed. If your URL or phone number changes in the future, you simply generate a fresh code and reprint.

Do these QR codes ever expire or have scan limits?

No. MyLogoQR codes never expire, have zero scan limits, and require no recurring subscriptions. Traditional dynamic QR vendors force links through proprietary redirect servers that shut off after free trials expire. Our client-side static codes connect mobile camera scanners directly to your target destination forever.

What logo size and contrast ratio is safe to prevent scan failures?

We recommend keeping your logo size slider between 20% and 25% of the total matrix area while selecting Error Correction Level Q (25% recovery) or Level H (30% recovery). Always ensure your logo has a clean outline or border that separates it from the surrounding QR dots, and test scan the live on-screen preview with both iOS and Android cameras before submitting files to physical print.

Is my data or Wi-Fi password stored on an external server?

No. MyLogoQR operates under a strict zero-knowledge, client-side architecture. All encoding, matrix generation, logo compositing, and vector rendering execute entirely within your device browser using in-memory JavaScript Canvas and SVG APIs. Your QR content is generated in your browser and is never uploaded. This website uses cookies for advertising and basic analytics, described in our Cookie Policy.

What is the new "Label under QR" feature for brands and restaurants?

Our optional Label under QR feature allows hospitality venues, fast food chains, retail stores, and corporate brands to add a clean, readable text label (such as "SCAN ME", "MENU", "CONNECT WI-FI", "ORDER HERE", or "PAY HERE") directly beneath the QR code. You can choose between a modern rounded Pill badge, clean typography with letter tracking, or a solid rectangular banner. When downloaded, the badge is seamlessly embedded into your high-resolution PNG or vector SVG export.

Can I use these QR codes for commercial products and business marketing?

Yes, 100%. All QR codes, vector SVG graphics, and high-resolution PNG files generated on MyLogoQR are completely free for commercial, retail, educational, and personal use without copyright royalties, licensing fees, or attribution requirements.

How do Wi-Fi QR codes connect guest devices automatically?

Our Wi-Fi tool structures network credentials into the standard Wi-Fi QR format (WIFI:T:WPA;S:name;P:password;;). When scanned with an iPhone or Android camera, the operating system decodes the protocol and displays a native prompt asking "Join [Network Name]?". Tapping Join automatically authenticates the device without typing passwords manually.

Which download format should I choose: PNG or SVG?

Use PNG (1000 x 1000 px) for digital presentations, social media, email signatures, website graphics, and standard office desktop printers. Choose SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics) for professional print shops, commercial packaging, restaurant acrylic stands, signage, and vector software like Adobe Illustrator or Figma.
