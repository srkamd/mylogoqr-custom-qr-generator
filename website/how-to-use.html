Reed-Solomon Level H

How Do Brand Logos Work Inside QR Codes?

When you put a logo in the center, you are covering up data pixels. MyLogoQR automatically uses Level H Error Correction (30% Redundancy). This discrete mathematical algorithm adds backup parity blocks so cameras can reconstruct the full link even if a large brand logo covers the middle.

🎯 Optimal Logo Size: 18% – 24%
⚡ Scan Speed: Under 50ms
Quality Checklist

Do's & Don'ts for 100% Reliable Scanning

Prevent expensive printing mistakes with these essential rules.

✅ DO THIS (Scans Instantly)

  • ✔️
    High Contrast: Always use dark foreground colors on a white or light background (at least 4.5:1 contrast).
  • ✔️
    Keep the Quiet Zone: Maintain a clear white margin around the outer edges so cameras can detect the border.
  • ✔️
    Export Vector SVG for Print: Use SVG vector for business cards, banners, and packaging so lines never blur.
  • ✔️
    Test on Two Real Phones: Always test-scan your design on an iPhone and an Android phone before mass-printing.

❌ AVOID THIS (Causes Scan Errors)

  • ✖️
    Inverted Colors: Avoid light yellow or white dots on dark backgrounds — millions of budget cameras fail to read them.
  • ✖️
    Covering the 3 Corner Squares: Never place your logo or artwork over the 3 large corner target squares.
  • ✖️
    Oversized Logos: Don't make your logo cover more than 30% of the surface area, or the code will corrupt.
  • ✖️
    Printing Too Small: Never print smaller than 2cm × 2cm for handheld items, or cameras cannot focus.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about creating and printing QR codes.

Will my generated QR codes ever expire or stop working?

Never. MyLogoQR generates true static QR codes. Your link or contact info is built directly into the visual dots. Because there is no middleman redirect server, your code can never be deactivated or expired.

Is there a scan limit on free codes?

No scan limits whatsoever. Whether scanned 5 times or 5 million times, your code works permanently at zero cost.

Which format is best for printing: PNG or SVG?

For printing on paper, business cards, merchandise, or billboards, always choose SVG. SVG is a vector format that scales infinitely without blurring. For websites, emails, or social media posts, PNG works great.

Does MyLogoQR upload my Wi-Fi password or contacts to a server?

No. Everything runs 100% inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript. Nothing is transmitted over the internet or saved to our servers.
