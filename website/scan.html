How to scan a QR code online without installing an app

Modern mobile phones and desktop computers do not require third-party app installations to decode QR codes. MyLogoQR provides a zero-installation, 100% in-browser decoder that accesses your camera hardware or image files directly via local JavaScript APIs.

Client-Side Security & Zero Server Storage

Traditional online scanners transmit your uploaded photos or camera frames to remote cloud servers for optical character recognition. MyLogoQR operates under a strict zero-knowledge architecture: camera frames are processed directly inside your browser memory using the Canvas API. No photos, URLs, Wi-Fi credentials, or contact details are ever sent to an external server.

Supported Formats

  • Website URLs: Instant link preview with secure https verification.
  • Wi-Fi Networks: Automatically parses SSID, encryption type (WPA/WPA2/WPA3), and network passwords.
  • Digital vCards: Decodes names, phone numbers, email addresses, and job titles.
  • WhatsApp Links: Direct chat phone numbers and prefilled messages.
