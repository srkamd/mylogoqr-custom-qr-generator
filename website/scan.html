What Does This QR Code Link To? How to Inspect & Decode Safely

MWritten by MyLogoQR Cyber Safety Team — Zero-Trust QR Inspection

Every day, millions of people point their mobile cameras at QR codes on parking meters, flyers, email attachments, and restaurant tables. However, blind scanning poses a serious security risk known as QR Phishing (Quishing): malicious actors paste fake stickers over legitimate barcodes to redirect unsuspecting users to credential-harvesting phishing sites.

MyLogoQR Link Inspector solves this by decoupling decoding from navigation. When you scan an image or point your camera here:

  1. We extract and display the raw decoded string on your screen first.
  2. You can inspect the full protocol (https:// vs http://) and root domain before taking action.
  3. We never automatically launch the URL or execute background redirects, keeping your device completely protected.

How to Identify QR Codes with Logos

Ever wondered why some QR codes have a small dinosaur, the letter 'C', or a company logo in the center? These are custom branded QR codes built using Error Correction Level H (30%). Under the ISO/IEC 18004 standard, up to 30% of the barcode's data blocks can be covered by a graphic without preventing cameras from decoding the remaining redundant data.

Security Tip: If a QR code directs you to a URL shortener (like bit.ly or tinyurl) or asks you to enter bank login credentials unexpectedly, do not proceed. Always verify the domain name matches the official company website.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does this QR code link to?

When you upload an image or scan a QR code with MyLogoQR, our scanner decodes and displays the exact raw destination URL on your screen without opening it automatically. This allows you to inspect the full domain name and query parameters to verify that the destination is legitimate and safe.

How do I scan a QR code from a picture or screenshot on my phone?

Simply click the 'Upload Image' tab on this page, choose the screenshot or photo from your gallery, and our in-browser reader will immediately decode the QR code and display its contents.

Is my camera feed or uploaded photo sent to any server?

No. The barcode detection and decoding happens 100% locally inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas and client-side JavaScript. No pictures or video frames are ever transmitted across the internet.

Can this scanner read QR codes with logos in the center?

Yes! Because standard QR codes with logos utilize Reed-Solomon Error Correction (up to 30% redundancy), our engine reconstructs obscured data modules and decodes branded codes with ease.

What barcode formats are supported besides QR codes?

In addition to standard QR codes, our engine can scan Micro QR codes, Data Matrix, Aztec codes, and standard 1D barcodes including UPC-A, EAN-13, and Code 128.
