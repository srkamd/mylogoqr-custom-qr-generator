Frequently Asked Inquiries

Quick answers to common questions before sending your message.

Is MyLogoQR truly free for commercial use?

Yes, 100%. You can generate unlimited QR codes and print them on commercial flyers, packaging, client websites, and restaurant tables with zero royalty fees and no attribution required.

Will my generated QR codes ever expire?

Never. MyLogoQR generates true static barcodes. The link or raw text is hardcoded directly into the visual pixel pattern. Because there is no redirect server in the middle, nothing can expire.

Do you offer customized enterprise white-label solutions?

Yes. If your company needs an on-premises QR generation micro-service, custom vector SVG styling, or integrated brand pipelines, reach out via the contact form above.
