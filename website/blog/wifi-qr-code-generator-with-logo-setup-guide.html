How Wi-Fi QR Codes Work Natively on iOS and Android

Both Apple iOS (Camera app) and Android (Google Lens / Native Camera) support the standard WIFI:T:WPA;S:NetworkName;P:Password;; protocol. When a guest points their phone camera at the code, a notification banner appears saying: 'Join [Network Name] Wi-Fi Network?' Tapping the prompt connects them automatically in less than two seconds.

Benefits of Wi-Fi QR Codes for Businesses

  • No More Typo Complaints: Complex passwords with uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols are entered flawlessly without human error.
  • Privacy Protection: Guests never need to see or write down your router's master password on scratch paper.
  • Enhanced Hospitality Branding: Display an elegant acrylic table tent with your cafe or restaurant logo in the center of the QR code.

Security Best Practices for Public Venues

Never share your internal administrative network or POS payment terminal Wi-Fi on a public QR code. Always create a dedicated 'Guest Wi-Fi' network with client isolation enabled on your router before printing QR table cards.

Generate your wireless code: Use our dedicated Free Wi-Fi QR Code Generator to export printable table tent graphics.
Print Material Warning: Never print QR codes on high-gloss UV varnishes or curved metallic surfaces. Camera flash and ceiling spotlight reflections create white glare over the corner markers, causing scanning vision algorithms to fail. Opt for matte cardstock or non-reflective vinyl.
Field-Tested Note: We always recommend running the 'Two-Phone Daylight Test' before sending any QR code to a commercial printer. Test scan the exported vector on both an iPhone and an older Android phone—first under direct bright sunlight, and then in a dimly lit hallway. If both open in under one second, your design is print-ready.
