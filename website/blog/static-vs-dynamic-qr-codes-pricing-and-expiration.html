The Infamous 'Free Trial' QR Code Trap Explained

Many online QR code generators lure users with promises of 'Free QR Codes'. They generate a dynamic redirect link that routes through their private servers. After 14 days or 50 scans, they deactivate the redirect and display a paywall error page to your customers—unless you pay an expensive recurring monthly fee!

What Is a Static QR Code (And Why They Never Expire)

At MyLogoQR, all codes generated are 100% Static:

  • Data is Hardcoded: Your destination URL, Wi-Fi password, or vCard details are written directly into the pixel pattern.
  • Zero Server Dependency: When a phone camera scans the code, it navigates directly to your website. Nothing passes through our servers.
  • Unlimited Lifetime Scans: A static QR code printed today will still work 10 years from now as long as your website exists. No monthly bills, no scan caps, and no expiration dates!

When Do You Actually Need a Dynamic QR Code?

Dynamic QR codes are only necessary if you require:

  1. Post-Print URL Changing: Changing where a printed billboard leads without physically replacing the billboard.
  2. Granular Scan Analytics: Tracking exact scan times, geographic cities, and mobile device types.

If you already own your website, you can easily track visitors by adding standard Google Analytics UTM parameters (e.g. ?utm_source=flyer) to your static QR code completely free!
